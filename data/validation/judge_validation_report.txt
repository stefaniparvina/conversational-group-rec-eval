Notes:
- 'kappa' shows quadratic-weighted kappa (suffix w) for the ordinal
  0-3 Process Quality dimensions, and unweighted Cohen's kappa
  otherwise. Verbal labels follow Landis & Koch (1977):
  <=.20 slight, <=.40 fair, <=.60 moderate, <=.80 substantial,
  >.80 almost perfect.
- The validation set is 15 transcripts. Per-dimension n is small (15),
  so the bootstrap CI is wide -- always report kappa WITH its CI, and
  lean on the pooled Process Quality row (n=60) as the headline.
- A judge that does not reach 'substantial' agreement on a metric is a
  finding to report and discuss, not a failure to hide.
- 'within1' = adjacent agreement: share of ordinal 0-3 score pairs
  agreeing within one point. 'PABAK' (Byrt et al. 1993) is a
  prevalence-adjusted agreement index for the binary metrics, where
  Cohen's kappa is distorted when one category dominates.
